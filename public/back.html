FAQ

How do I activate my key?

After checkout you land on the claim page with your session_id in the URL. Your key is fetched from /api/license/claim and shown in a box — copy it, then run entheai activate ENTH-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX once in your terminal. Done.

Is there a subscription?

No. This is a single one-time payment of €9.99. No renewal, no recurring charge, no account required to keep what you paid for.

What happens if I lose my key?

Keys are tied to the email used at checkout. If you lose yours, reach out via entheai.com and it will be re-issued once the purchase is verified. Until then, the free MIT builds keep working.
