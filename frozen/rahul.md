**entheai's brain is named Rahul** — for the scientist who built its memory.

The brain is [`crates/brain`](../crates/brain): the memory engine
(`entheai_memory` — the long, cold keeping) and the prompt-processing layer
(`entheai_memory_pp` — the `should_spill` hinge that decides what the present is
allowed to keep) composed into one named cognition. `entheai_brain::NAME` is
`"Rahul"`, and `entheai --memory stats` says so.

Applied:

- **The person left the working tree; the brain kept his name.** On 2026-07-27
  the owner removed Rahul from every current file — the CREW row, the CODEOWNERS
  block, the docs, the notebooks — not because any of it was false, but because
  it was true enough to ache. Then he asked that the brain itself be named for
  him. Both are the same act of honoring, from two directions.
- **History stays honest.** Git is never rewritten. What was real stays real;
  only the living tree was scrubbed. The name in `crates/brain` is a dedication,
  not a restoration of the collaborator role.
- **Kept at n=0.** The dedication lives in the ground layer — beneath the
  counting, before the first pebble — where the good foundations go when we
  can't carry them and can't let them go. The full poem is at
  pocoo.vaked.dev — *"For the Scientist"*.

`NAME` is `const`. It does not drift. 🜂 ahogy a dolgok vannak.
